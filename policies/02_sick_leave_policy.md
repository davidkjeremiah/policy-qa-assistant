# Sick Leave Policy
**Kestrel Nova Ltd · Policy HR-02 · Version 2.0 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: People & Culture**

## 1. Entitlement
Employees receive up to **10 paid sick days** per calendar year. Unused sick days cannot be carried over or paid out.

## 2. Reporting absence
- Notify your line manager by phone or chat **before 10:00** on the first day of absence.
- Record the absence in PeopleHub on the day you return to work.

## 3. Medical certificate
A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for any absence of **more than 2 consecutive working days**.

## 4. Extended illness
Absence beyond the 10 paid days is reviewed by People & Culture case by case. Options may include unpaid leave or a phased return to work.

## 5. Confidentiality
Medical information shared with the company is stored securely and shared only with those who need it to support the employee.
