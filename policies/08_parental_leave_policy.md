# Parental Leave Policy
**Kestrel Nova Ltd · Policy HR-04 · Version 2.1 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: People & Culture**

## 1. Maternity leave
Birth mothers receive **16 weeks** of maternity leave at full pay.

## 2. Paternity leave
Fathers and partners receive **10 working days** of paternity leave at full pay. It must be taken within **3 months** of the child's birth or adoption and may be taken in up to two blocks.

## 3. Adoption leave
The primary carer of an adopted child receives the same leave as maternity leave (16 weeks at full pay). The secondary carer receives the same leave as paternity leave.

## 4. Notice
Employees should inform People & Culture at least 8 weeks before the planned start of leave where possible.

## 5. Return to work
Employees returning from maternity or adoption leave may work a reduced schedule of 4 days per week at full pay for their first 4 weeks back.
