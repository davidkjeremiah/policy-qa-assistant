# Learning and Development Policy
**Kestrel Nova Ltd · Policy HR-07 · Version 1.2 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: People & Culture**

## 1. Annual budget
Each permanent employee has a learning budget of **NGN 400,000 per calendar year** for courses, certifications, conferences and books. Unused budget does not carry over.

## 2. Approval
Learning spend must be approved by the line manager **before** booking. Requests are submitted in PeopleHub with the course name, cost and expected benefit.

## 3. Study leave
Employees receive **3 paid study days per year** to attend exams or courses. Study days are separate from annual leave.

## 4. Repayment
If the company pays for a single course or certification costing **more than NGN 200,000**, and the employee resigns within **12 months** of completing it, the employee must repay **50%** of the cost. The amount is deducted from the final salary where possible.
