# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy
**Kestrel Nova Ltd · Policy HR-05 · Version 1.4 · Effective 1 March 2026 · Owner: People & Culture**

## 1. Hybrid working model
- Employees in hybrid roles work from the Lagos office at least **2 days per week**.
- **Tuesday and Thursday** are anchor days: all hybrid staff are expected in the office on these days.
- Other days may be worked from home with the line manager's agreement.

## 2. Fully remote periods
Employees may work fully remotely for up to **2 weeks (10 working days) per calendar year**, for example while visiting family. This requires line manager approval in PeopleHub at least 5 working days in advance. Working remotely from outside Nigeria also requires approval from People & Culture for tax and legal reasons.

## 3. Home working allowance
Hybrid employees receive an internet and utilities allowance of **NGN 25,000 per month**, paid with salary.

## 4. Security when working remotely
- Company systems must be accessed through the company VPN.
- Work must not be done on public Wi-Fi without the VPN.
- Screens must be locked when unattended. See also the IT Equipment and Security Policy (IT-01).
