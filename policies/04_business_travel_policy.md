# Business Travel Policy
**Kestrel Nova Ltd · Policy FIN-03 · Version 4.1 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: Finance**

## 1. Approval
All business travel requires line manager approval before booking.

## 2. Booking
- All flights and hotels are booked through the Travel Desk (travel@kestrelnova.example).
- Domestic travel must be requested at least **7 days** before departure.
- International travel must be requested at least **21 days** before departure.
- Late requests may be refused or may need Director approval.

## 3. Class of travel
- Economy class for all flights of **6 hours or less**.
- For flights longer than 6 hours, **Director level and above** may travel business class. Other employees travel premium economy where available, otherwise economy.

## 4. Accommodation
- Domestic hotels: maximum **NGN 80,000 per night**.
- International hotels: maximum **USD 180 per night** unless the Travel Desk confirms no suitable option is available.

## 5. Per diem (daily allowance)
- Domestic travel: **NGN 30,000 per day**.
- International travel: **USD 60 per day**.
- The per diem covers meals and local incidentals. It is paid for each night spent away from home.

## 6. Claims
Travel costs not booked by the Travel Desk (for example taxis) are claimed under the Expense Reimbursement Policy (FIN-01).
