# IT Equipment and Security Policy
**Kestrel Nova Ltd · Policy IT-01 · Version 2.3 · Effective 1 February 2026 · Owner: IT**

## 1. Equipment
- Each employee receives a company laptop. Laptops are replaced every **3 years**, or earlier if faulty.
- Company equipment remains company property and must be returned when employment ends.

## 2. Loss or theft
Report a lost or stolen device to IT Support (it-support@kestrelnova.example) **within 24 hours**. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. Theft must also be reported to the police, and the report reference shared with IT.

## 3. Passwords and access
- Passwords must be at least **12 characters** long.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for email and all company systems.
- Passwords must never be shared, including with IT staff.

## 4. Removable media
Personal USB drives and external hard drives must not be connected to company laptops. Company-issued encrypted drives may be requested from IT.

## 5. Software
Only software approved by IT may be installed. Requests are made through the IT service portal.
