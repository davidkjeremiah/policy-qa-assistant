# Code of Conduct: Gifts and Hospitality
**Kestrel Nova Ltd · Policy CMP-02 · Version 1.1 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: Compliance**

## 1. Principle
Employees must not accept gifts or hospitality that could influence, or appear to influence, business decisions.

## 2. Gifts
- Gifts with a value of up to **NGN 15,000** may be accepted without declaration.
- Gifts above NGN 15,000 must be **declared to Compliance within 5 working days** (compliance@kestrelnova.example). Compliance decides whether the gift may be kept, returned or donated.
- Cash, vouchers and gift cards must never be accepted, whatever the value.

## 3. Hospitality
Business meals with clients or suppliers are acceptable if they are occasional and reasonable. Hospitality from a supplier during an active tender must be refused.

## 4. Reporting concerns
Concerns can be raised confidentially with Compliance or through the ethics line (ethics@kestrelnova.example). Retaliation against anyone raising a concern in good faith is not tolerated.
