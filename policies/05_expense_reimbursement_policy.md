# Expense Reimbursement Policy
**Kestrel Nova Ltd · Policy FIN-01 · Version 5.0 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: Finance**

## 1. General rule
Employees are reimbursed for reasonable costs incurred for company business.

## 2. Submitting claims
- Submit claims in the Spendly expense tool within **30 days** of the expense.
- Each item must include a receipt. Items without a receipt are only paid if the amount is below NGN 5,000 and a short explanation is given.

## 3. Approvals
- Items up to NGN 50,000: line manager approval.
- Items **above NGN 50,000**: line manager **and** Finance approval.

## 4. Payment
Approved claims submitted **before the 20th** of the month are paid with that month's payroll. Claims submitted on or after the 20th are paid in the following month's payroll.

## 5. Limits and exclusions
- Client meals: up to **NGN 20,000 per person**, with the names of attendees listed on the claim.
- **Alcohol is not reimbursable.**
- Traffic fines and parking penalties are not reimbursable.
- Personal entertainment (films, minibar, gym) is not reimbursable.
