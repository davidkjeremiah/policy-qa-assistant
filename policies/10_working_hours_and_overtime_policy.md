# Working Hours and Overtime Policy
**Kestrel Nova Ltd · Policy HR-03 · Version 2.0 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: People & Culture**

## 1. Working week
The standard working week is **40 hours**, Monday to Friday.

## 2. Core hours
Employees must be available between **10:00 and 16:00** (West Africa Time) on working days. Start and finish times outside core hours are flexible, with line manager agreement.

## 3. Overtime
- Employees in **grades 1–4** are paid overtime at 1.5 times their hourly rate, when the overtime is approved in advance.
- Employees in **grade 5 and above** are not paid overtime. They receive **time off in lieu (TOIL)** at a rate of 1 hour off for each hour of approved extra work.
- TOIL must be used within **2 months** or it is lost.

## 4. Weekend work
Weekend work must be approved in advance by the line manager, except in a declared production incident.
