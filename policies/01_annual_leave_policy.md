# Annual Leave Policy
**Kestrel Nova Ltd · Policy HR-01 · Version 3.2 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: People & Culture**

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how much paid annual leave employees receive and how to request it.

## 2. Eligibility
All permanent employees are entitled to annual leave. Employees on probation (the first **3 months** of employment) earn leave but may only take it with written approval from their line manager.

## 3. Entitlement
- Full-time employees receive **20 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year after completing probation.
- Employees with more than 5 years of continuous service receive **25 working days**.
- Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours.
- Public holidays are in addition to annual leave.

## 4. Requesting leave
- All leave must be requested through the PeopleHub portal.
- Requests for more than 3 consecutive working days must be submitted at least **10 working days** in advance.
- Requests for 3 days or fewer should be submitted at least 2 working days in advance.
- Leave is not confirmed until the line manager approves it in PeopleHub.

## 5. Carrying over leave
- Up to **5 unused days** may be carried over into the next calendar year.
- Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**; any carried-over days not used by then are lost.
- Days above the 5-day limit are lost at the end of the year unless the Head of People approves an exception in writing.

## 6. Leaving the company
When employment ends, any accrued but unused annual leave for the current year is **paid out at the employee's basic daily rate** in the final salary. Leave taken in excess of the accrued amount is deducted from the final salary.
